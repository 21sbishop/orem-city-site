Resident Services

The City of Orem provides dependable services to nearly 100,000 residents. From clean water to safe streets, our departments work every day to keep Utah Valley’s Family City running.

Trash & Recycling

Curbside collection runs weekly. Recycling is picked up every other week on the same day as trash.

  • Request a new cart

Permits & Licenses

Planning a fence, deck, or new business? Most permits can be started online and reviewed in five business days.

City Council

The seven-member City Council meets on the first and third Tuesday of each month at 6:30 p.m. in Council Chambers. Meetings are open to the public and streamed live.
