Design Library

The shared foundations for every City of Orem screen: brand colors, a grey scale, one type family, and the city logo mark.

Version 1.0 · 2610 Project

Use these tokens as written. Every color, size, and logo on a City of Orem page should come from this library.

Colors

Primary teal carries headings, links, and navigation. Secondary amber is an accent for highlights, bullets, and focus rings. Never set body text in amber on white.

Typography

Work Sans is the single type family for the city. It is open, friendly, and easy to read on phones, which suits residents of every age looking up city information.

Logo Mark

The mark pairs Mount Timpanogos with the morning sun. Use the light version on white or gray/050, and the dark version on gray/900.

Logo Rules

  • Keep clear space equal to the sun's width on every side
  • Show the mark no smaller than 48 pixels wide
  • Never recolor, stretch, or rotate the mountains
